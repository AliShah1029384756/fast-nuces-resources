The Ultimate Academic & Career Transition Hub

One-stop platform for entry tests, scholarships, jobs, and templates.

1. Project Overview & Objectives

This website is a one-stop resource platform focused on easing academic and career transitions for:

  • College graduates (FSc/A-Levels) preparing for university admissions and entry tests
  • University graduates (BS/MS) seeking jobs, freelancing, or higher studies

Key Objectives

  • Aggregate authentic entry test material in one place
  • Provide step-by-step guidance for fully-funded scholarships
  • Offer MTO tracks, ATS-friendly CV templates, and skill roadmaps for job seekers

2. Architecture & Core Modules

The site is split into three dashboards for easy navigation.

Module A: College-to-University Bridge (Pre-Uni Hub)

  • Entrance Exam Preparation: syllabus, past papers (NUST, FAST, GIKI, UET), curated playlists.
  • Admission Intelligence: live admission calendar and merit calculators.
  • Transition Course: basic programming and productivity tools guide.

Module B: Graduate Career & Higher Studies Hub (Post-Uni Hub)

  • Global Scholarships Database with SOP, LOR, and application guides.
  • Corporate & Job Readiness: MTO trackers, interview kits, and practice links.
  • Freelancing & Remote Work: payment gateway guides and profile optimization.

Module C: The Vault (Downloads & Templates)

  • Editable CV templates, test-prep material, and cold email templates.

3. Recommended Tech Stack

  • Frontend: Next.js (React) + Tailwind CSS
  • Content: Markdown (MDX) for easy content updates
  • Hosting: Vercel / Netlify for CI/CD
